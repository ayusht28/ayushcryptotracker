HOW TO WIRE UP MULTIPLE PORTFOLIOS BACKEND

1. Copy backend/portfolios.js into services/gateway/src/routes/portfolios.js

2. In services/gateway/src/index.js add these two lines:

   Near the top with the other route imports:
       const portfoliosRoutes = require('./routes/portfolios');

   Near the other app.use route mounts:
       app.use('/api/portfolios', portfoliosRoutes);

3. Add the contents of gateway-additions.js to the bottom of frontend/src/api/gateway.js

That's it. The /api/portfolios/list, /create, /rename, /delete endpoints will be live.
